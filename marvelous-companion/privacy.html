Privacy Policy

Marvelous Companion

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Marvelous Companion doesn't require an account, and everything you track in the app stays on your device. The one exception is crash reporting: when the app crashes or runs into a serious error, it sends a diagnostic report so we can find and fix the problem.

Data That Stays on Your Device

Your collection (owned, in-bag, and painted figures), your settings, and anything you search for are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service from Google, to report crashes and serious errors. A report includes:

  • Technical details of the crash or error, such as the stack trace and the error message.
  • Device information, such as the device model, operating system version, available memory and storage, screen orientation, whether the app was in the background, and whether the device is rooted or jailbroken.
  • The app version.
  • Random identifiers generated by Crashlytics and Firebase for this installation of the app. They are used to group reports, and they aren't linked to your name or any account.
  • A short log of recent app events written by the app itself, such as "app started" or a note that some game reference data was missing. These logs never include what you type, what you search for, or your collection.

Crash reporting is turned on in all released versions of the app.

What We Don't Collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, collection, or search terms. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no usage analytics. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data.

How Crash Data Is Used

Crash reports are used only to diagnose crashes and improve the app's stability. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Data Retention

Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their associated identifiers for 90 days, then begins deleting them from its live and backup systems.

Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the app deletes your collection and settings from your device.

Crash reports aren't linked to your name, email, or any account, so we can't identify which reports came from a particular person. Instead, they are deleted automatically after the 90-day retention period described above. If you have questions about your data, contact us at support@punkforge.dev.

Changes to This Policy

If what the app collects changes, we'll update this policy and the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@punkforge.dev.
